Research

PhD project: Population-based reliability assessment of buildings

This PhD studies the structural safety of buildings in probabilistic terms, i.e., from a reliability perspective. Building codes are calibrated this way at component level, but little guidance exists on assessing how buildings perform as structural systems. To address this gap, we will first explore how to upscale system reliability analyses to the building level. Then, we will investigate how to exploit different sources of data to refine reliability estimates trough Bayesian updating. And eventually, we want to push beyond the single building, transferring what we learn across populations of similar structures.

The focus is on multi-story timber buildings due to their growing popularity and the unique challenges they present in terms of structural reliability.

The project is supervised by Eleni Chatzi at ETH Zürich and Raphaël Steenbergen at TNO.
